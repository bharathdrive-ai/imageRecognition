Privacy Policy

Image Text Recognition · Last updated 12 October 2026

Image Text Recognition is a free tool that reads text from images and PDFs. All recognition happens in your web browser. Your files are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Files you add

Images and PDFs you open, drop, paste or photograph are processed in your browser's memory. They are not sent to any server and are gone when you close or reload the page.

Google Drive

If you choose Google Drive, you sign in with Google, which asks you to grant the drive.file permission. That permission lets the app open only the files you pick in the Google file picker, nothing else in your Drive. Those files download directly from Google's Drive API into your browser.

  • We don't store, copy, share or sell your Google data.
  • We don't modify or delete files in your Drive.
  • The sign-in token stays in your browser's memory only for the session.
  • You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What your browser stores

Your recognition settings and light/dark theme choice are saved in your browser's local storage. Downloaded language models are cached by your browser so the next run starts faster. You can clear both by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Third-party services

To run, the page loads code and fonts from these services, which may see your IP address as part of a normal web request:

  • jsDelivr: the OCR engine, language models and PDF reader
  • Google Fonts: the page's fonts
  • Google Identity Services and Google Picker: only when you use the Google Drive button
  • Vercel or GitHub Pages: hosting for the page itself

The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
